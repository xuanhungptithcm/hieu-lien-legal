Privacy Policy

Hiểu Liền Studio · Last updated: 24 October 2026

1. Who uses the app

Hiểu Liền Studio is a private, self-hosted tool used only by the owner of the Hiểu Liền channel. It has no public sign-up and does not collect data from other people.

2. Data we receive from TikTok

  • Basic profile (scope user.info.basic): open_id, display name and avatar, used only to show which TikTok account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens, used only to upload videos that the owner has approved (scope video.upload).

We do not read followers, messages, other users' content or any other TikTok data.

3. Where data is stored

All data stays on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Videos are sent only to TikTok, and only when the owner clicks the send button for that video.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner disconnects TikTok in the app's Settings, which deletes them immediately. Access can also be revoked in TikTok › Settings and privacy › Security › Manage app permissions.

5. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
