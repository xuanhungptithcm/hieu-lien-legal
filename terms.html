1. What the app is

Hiểu Liền Studio is a private, self-hosted tool that creates short Vietnamese explainer videos for the Hiểu Liền channel and lets the channel owner send a finished video to their own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public; the only user is the owner of the Hiểu Liền accounts.

2. Connecting TikTok

You connect your TikTok account through TikTok's own authorization page (Login Kit). You can disconnect at any time in the app's Settings or in TikTok › Settings and privacy › Security › Manage app permissions. Disconnecting deletes the stored access token.

3. Posting

The app never posts on its own. A video is sent to TikTok only after you review it and click the send button for that video. You are responsible for the content you post and for following the TikTok Community Guidelines and TikTok Terms of Service.
