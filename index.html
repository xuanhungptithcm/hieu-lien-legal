Hiểu Liền Studio

Công nghệ khó? Xem là hiểu liền.

What it is

Hiểu Liền Studio is the private, self-hosted tool behind the Hiểu Liền channel. It turns a topic into a short Vietnamese explainer video (script, voice, visuals, music, caption and hashtags).

How it works with TikTok

  1. The channel owner connects their TikTok account through TikTok's authorization page.
  2. For each finished video, the owner checks the preview, caption and hashtags, then clicks "Send to TikTok".
  3. The video arrives as a draft in the owner's TikTok inbox; the owner edits and posts it in the TikTok app.

Nothing is sent without the owner's click, and the app is not offered to other users.
